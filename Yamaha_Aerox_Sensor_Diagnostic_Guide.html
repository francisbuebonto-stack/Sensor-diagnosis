Recording, diagnosis and history now work; accessibility, contrast and layout fixed.

  • Record saves readings; the health score, Auto Diagnosis and Session History use them. The score shows -- until data exists.
  • New Session archives the current readings (last 50 kept) and clears the form.

⚠ Open items to verify against the service manual

  • O2 range: 0.2–0.8 V (Sensor Input) vs 0.3–0.7 V (Fuel tab and checklist).
  • ECT range: 75–92 °C (Sensor Input) vs 80–95 °C (Fuel checklist) and 80 °C+ (Fuel tab).
  • MAP wording says output drops at high load; most MAP sensors rise with manifold pressure.
  • ISC checklist suggests brake cleaner; confirm which cleaner the manual allows.
  • "Measures precisely 12.2 Ω": confirm the tolerance and the temperature.
  • Tagged unverified: connector pins and wire colors, 28 mm bore, filter pressure drop, ISC winding 20–30 Ω, CKP 185–275 Ω and air gap, O2 100 ms limit.
  • The health score is a simple count (optimal 1, warning 0.5, critical 0), not a Yamaha figure.
