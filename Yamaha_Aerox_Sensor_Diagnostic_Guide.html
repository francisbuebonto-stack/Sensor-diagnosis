💨 Air System Flow & Throttle Body Assembly

Based on Yamaha Service Manual GDR155/GDR155-A

Air enters engine via Air Filter Case ➔ Air Filter Case Joint ➔ Throttle Body Assembly (Integrated Single Unit) ➔ Intake Manifold ➔ Engine Cylinder.

Critical Understanding: ISC Integrated in Throttle Body

ISC is built directly into the throttle body assembly (not mounted on the intake manifold). Plunger stroke: 0.0–3.0 mm. Maintains 1500–1700 r/min idle when throttle is fully closed.

Throttle Body Sensor Assembly (Item 19) Specs

  • Item 22 (TPS): Closed: 13–21 mV, Open: 97–107 mV
  • Item 20 (MAP): Output: 3.88–4.12 V at 101.3 kPa (sea level)
  • Item 21 (IAT): Resistance: 5850–6150 Ω at 0°C

⛽ Fuel System Path & Injector Delivery

Based on Yamaha Service Manual GDR155/GDR155-A

Fuel Tank (Fuel Pump Assembly [Item 31] regulated output: 220–300 kPa) ➔ Fuel Filter ➔ Fuel Hose #1 ➔ Fuel Injector [Item 30] (12.2 Ω solenoid resistance, 10–20 ms pulse width at warm idle) ➔ Combustion.

Key Fuel System Sensors

  • Coolant Temperature Sensor (ECT - Item 18): Cold (<80°C) enriches fuel; Warm (80°C+) leans fuel. Spec: 2512–2777 Ω @ 20°C; 210–221 Ω @ 100°C.
  • O2 Sensor (Item 23): Measures oxygen in exhaust. Range: 0.3–0.7 V oscillating at idle.

⚡ Ignition System Flow & Spark Timing

Based on Yamaha Service Manual GDR155/GDR155-A

Battery (12V, 6.0 Ah) ➔ Main Switch ➔ Ignition Fuse (7.5 A) ➔ Safety Interlock (Sidestand Switch must be UP) ➔ ECU Timing Inputs (AC Magneto + Crankshaft Position Sensor [Item 26]) ➔ Ignition Coil (Primary: 2.16–2.64 Ω, Secondary: 8.64–12.96 kΩ) ➔ Spark Plug Cap (3.75–6.25 kΩ) ➔ Spark Plug (Item 28, gap: 0.8–0.9 mm) ➔ Spark in Cylinder.

Multimeter Quick-Check Resistance Spec Table

  • Ignition Coil Primary: 2.16–2.64 Ω
  • Ignition Coil Secondary: 8.64–12.96 kΩ
  • Spark Plug Cap: 3.75–6.25 kΩ
  • Spark Plug Gap: 0.8–0.9 mm
